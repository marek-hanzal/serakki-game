# Serakki Privacy Policy

Canonical page: https://serakki.dev/privacy/

Last updated 2026-10-04

The Serakki website does not track you, and Serakki Mini, the full game and the Editor do not send me telemetry or automatic crash reports. Hosting providers process requests to deliver the website and downloads. I receive personal data if you choose to contact me or send me files.

## 2. Website: no tracking or cookies

The website has no analytics, advertising trackers, tracking pixels, fingerprinting or session recording. It does not set cookies or write visitor data to local storage, session storage or IndexedDB. There are no visitor accounts or contact forms. Fonts and illustrations are served with the website, without external embeds.

Interactive previews run in your browser. Their temporary project and gameplay state stays in memory and is discarded when the page reloads or the preview closes. Your browser may cache ordinary website files; this is not a visitor profile or tracking database maintained by me.

## 3. Hosting and downloads

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub receives the technical information needed to serve a request, including your IP address. GitHub states that visitors' IP addresses are logged and stored for security, whether or not they are signed in. I do not receive or have access to these visitor logs.

Mini download links lead to GitHub Releases. Following them sends a request to GitHub, whose privacy notice applies to its delivery and logging. I do not maintain a visitor or download history identifying you.

[GitHub Pages: data collection](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/what-is-github-pages) · [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)

## 4. Offline applications and local files

Serakki Mini, the full game and the Editor do not automatically send me usage data, gameplay history, diagnostic reports or your files. Playing does not require a Serakki account. Saves, settings, authored projects and imported resources are stored locally, not uploaded to a Serakki service.

The applications automatically write local technical logs for troubleshooting. A failed game session can also leave a local incident snapshot with game data. These files stay on your device unless you share them. Local logging is separate from the voluntary Export Diagnostics action described below.

Your operating system, store client or a backup/synchronisation service you use may process local files under its own settings. Optional Editor agent connections are described in section 9.

## 5. What Export Diagnostics contains

Export Diagnostics in the application menu opens a save dialog and creates a ZIP file at the location you choose. Cancelling creates no archive. Exporting does not upload the ZIP, send an email or notify me. You decide whether to inspect, keep, delete or send it.

The archive contains the following:

- A manifest with the export time, application version, operating-system platform and release, CPU architecture, and display dimensions and scale factor.
- Filtered support logs: timestamps, severity and technical summaries; for official games, session identifiers, game/package versions, gameplay events and performance or failure information needed to reproduce a problem. Session identifiers are not an account or a persistent player identifier.
- If your last played game was an official game: its package identifier, version and content hash, plus its available save slots and backups. Saves contain gameplay progress and state.
- If your last played game was a community game: only that classification is recorded in the manifest; community saves are not included.

## 6. What the standard export leaves out

The standard ZIP does not include game packages or artwork/audio bundles, private Editor projects, community saves, detailed application logs, MCP tool logs or the local incident snapshot. It includes only the support-log files and official-game saves described above.

Detailed error bodies are omitted from the support log. Structured support events redact fields designated for email addresses, paths, project identifiers, credentials, tokens and raw errors. The export is designed for technical troubleshooting, not to collect contact details or sensitive personal data. This filtering is not a guarantee that every possible piece of personal information is removed. A report can also become personal data when linked to the person who sends it.

Review files before sharing them. Detailed local logs or files you attach separately may contain filesystem paths, project content or other information that the standard export excludes. Do not send passwords, access tokens or unnecessary personal information.

## 7. Contact and voluntary support

If you email me, I receive your email address, any name you provide, the message, ordinary email metadata and any attachments you send. The support@serakki.dev mailbox is provided by Webglobe, s.r.o., a Czech provider, which processes email content and delivery data to operate the mailbox.

If you contact me on Discord, I receive the account name, messages and attachments you choose to share. Messages in public community channels can also be read by other members. Discord processes platform data under its own privacy policy.

Contact and diagnostic sharing are voluntary. You can browse and play without sending them. Without information relevant to a request, I may be unable to investigate it. This policy does not create a support entitlement beyond the license or applicable law.

[Webglobe Privacy Policy](https://www.webglobe.cz/gdpr) · [Discord Privacy Policy](https://discord.com/privacy)

## 8. Purposes and legal grounds

I use submitted information only as needed to answer enquiries, investigate technical problems, fix defects, handle complaints and privacy requests, or establish, exercise or defend legal claims. I do not sell personal data or use support correspondence for advertising. I do not make decisions about you through automated decision-making or profiling.

Under the GDPR, the legal grounds depend on the request: legitimate interests in responding to enquiries, troubleshooting and protecting legal claims (Article 6(1)(f)); performing a contract or taking steps you request before a contract (Article 6(1)(b)), where applicable; and complying with legal obligations (Article 6(1)(c)). Browsing, downloading or sending a message is not treated as blanket consent to personal-data processing.

## 9. Optional agents and external services

The Editor can connect to an agent through MCP if you enable and configure it. A connected client can receive project information, artwork previews and Board screenshots, and use the tools you make available. A remote connection can also involve a tunnel provider. Those connections are your explicit choice; they are not telemetry sent to me. The connected agent and other providers may process the supplied content under their own terms and privacy policies.

Links to Discord, YouTube, Steam, GitHub and other websites open external services. Store purchases and accounts are handled by the store. These providers process information under their own privacy notices when you use their services.

[Steam Privacy Policy](https://store.steampowered.com/privacy_agreement/)

## 10. Recipients and international processing

Information you send is available to me and to the providers needed to deliver and store that communication, including Webglobe for email and Discord when you use it. I may disclose necessary information if required by law or to resolve a legal claim. I do not publish your submitted diagnostic archive as part of routine support.

Webglobe's published notice describes recipients in Czechia, the EU or countries covered by an adequacy decision. GitHub and Discord describe international processing, including in the United States, and their transfer safeguards in their privacy notices. This does not mean that all data handled by external services stays in the EU.

Any transfer I arrange of personal data outside the European Economic Area must comply with the GDPR, using an applicable adequacy decision or appropriate safeguards. You can contact me for information about the recipients and safeguards applicable to your data, including a copy of relevant safeguards where applicable.

## 11. How long information is kept

Local application logs rotate within fixed size and file-count limits; an incident snapshot is replaced by a later failed session. Exported ZIPs, saves and projects remain on your device until you remove them or your own storage system does so. I cannot remotely access or delete those files.

For information you send me, retention depends on whether the enquiry or investigation remains open, whether follow-up is needed and whether the information is still necessary to resolve the problem. I delete personal data when it is no longer needed for these purposes. Material needed for a legal obligation or claim may be kept for the applicable statutory retention or limitation period. Where possible, a technical defect record is kept without unnecessary sender information.

Hosting and communication providers apply their own retention and backup rules. I cannot access or delete GitHub Pages visitor logs; requests about GitHub's own processing should be addressed to GitHub.

You may object to processing based on legitimate interests on grounds relating to your situation. These rights are subject to the conditions and exceptions in applicable law.

Send requests to support@serakki.dev or my postal address in section 1. I will respond without undue delay, normally within one month; a lawful extension will be explained. I may need proportionate information to confirm your identity. The discretionary nature of game support does not limit my privacy-law obligations.

You can complain to a supervisory authority, including the Czech Office for Personal Data Protection (Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů), Pplk. Sochora 27, 170 00 Praha 7, Czechia, or the authority in your place of habitual residence, work or the alleged infringement.
