What if a little idea
got a day job?

Trees become timber. Plans become workshops. One useful thing makes the next one possible. Before you know it, your little world has a rhythm of its own.

Opening the workshop…

Click an ingredient to bring it over. Right-click to put it back. Click a recipe to plan a batch — up to three at a time.

What if the world
worked your way?

Change what happens when things meet. Invent a creature, a recipe, a wonderfully unreasonable economy. The Editor comes with Serakki — the same world is yours to play with, pull apart and make into something new.

Make your own kind of world ↗

What if you had a second pair of hands? Connect ChatGPT through the Editor’s MCP connection. Describe an idea, build it together, then see what it actually does. Bring your agent ↗

What if you
stayed a while?

No rush. Play offline, keep your progress, come back to your little world. Serakki is free to play during the playtest, ahead of Steam Early Access.
